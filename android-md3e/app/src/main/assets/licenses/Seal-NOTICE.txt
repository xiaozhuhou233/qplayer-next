Copyright 2021 SOUP and Apache License 2.0 notice in Md3eSealSharedAxis.kt.

QPlayer adaptation uses music and AI-generation states instead of Seal's
download tasks. The Seal downloader, URL configuration and format selection
are not part of this adaptation. Image decoding remains QPlayer's loader;
the image appearance animation follows Seal AsyncImageImpl's crossfade.
