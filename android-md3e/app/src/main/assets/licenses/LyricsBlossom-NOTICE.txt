Lyrics presentation reference: user-supplied ui.zip (Lyrics Blossom).

QPlayer's native lyric presentation ports the lyric-specific spring,
cascade, word-group and instrumental-break algorithms from
source_algorithms.js, and presentation behavior from app.js/styles.css.
TTML/LYS vocal channels, translations and romanization use QPlayer's
native lyric model. The reference's bundled Apple fonts are not included.
